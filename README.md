# 🧠 CS50AI – My Journey Through Harvard's AI Course

Welcome to my repo for [CS50's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/2020/)! This course explores how AI works under the hood — from search algorithms and logic to machine learning and neural networks. Below are all the projects I tackled as part of the course, with quick explanations of what each one’s about.

---

## 📁 Projects Overview

### 🔍 degrees/
**Topic:** Search  
**What I did:** Built an algorithm to find the shortest connection between any two actors via the movies they've starred in together — kind of like “Six Degrees of Kevin Bacon,” but programmatic. Used breadth-first search to get the shortest path.

### 🧬 heredity/
**Topic:** Probability  
**What I did:** Calculated the probability of people having certain genes and traits based on genetic inheritance. This involved a joint probability distribution over all family members.

### 🤖 tictactoe/
**Topic:** Game Playing & Minimax  
**What I did:** Created an unbeatable AI to play Tic Tac Toe using the Minimax algorithm. It looks ahead at all possible outcomes and always picks the optimal move.

### 🕵️‍♂️ knights/
**Topic:** Knowledge Representation  
**What I did:** Solved logic puzzles where characters (knights and knaves) either always tell the truth or always lie. I used propositional logic to deduce who's who based on their statements.

### 🧠 minesweeper/
**Topic:** Logical Inference  
**What I did:** Programmed an AI that can play Minesweeper using logic and inference. It builds knowledge about which cells are safe or mines based on the revealed numbers.

### 💣 nim/
**Topic:** Reinforcement Learning  
**What I did:** Taught an AI to play the game of Nim using Q-learning. The AI plays against itself thousands of times and eventually figures out a winning strategy without being explicitly told the rules.

### 📈 pagerank/
**Topic:** Probability & Link Analysis  
**What I did:** Implemented the PageRank algorithm (like Google does) to rank pages based on how many other pages link to them. I did it both iteratively and using sampling.

### 🧩 crossword/
**Topic:** Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs)  
**What I did:** Created an AI to generate crossword puzzles, making sure that all the words fit properly by satisfying constraints like word length, intersections, etc.

### 🧪 parser/
**Topic:** Natural Language Processing (NLP)  
**What I did:** Built a sentence parser using context-free grammar to break down English sentences into their grammatical parts (noun phrases, verb phrases, etc.).

### 🛍️ shopping/
**Topic:** Machine Learning  
**What I did:** Used a k-nearest neighbor classifier to predict whether a user will make a purchase based on their browsing behavior on a shopping site.

### 🚦 traffic/
**Topic:** Neural Networks  
**What I did:** Trained a convolutional neural network (CNN) using TensorFlow to recognize traffic signs from images. This one used real-world image data and deep learning.

### 📢 attention/
**Topic:** Transformers / BERT  
**What I did:** Played around with pre-trained BERT models and visualized how the attention mechanism works — basically seeing which words BERT focuses on when processing language.
